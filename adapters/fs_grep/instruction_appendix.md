## Reading a `memory/` note

Each file under `memory/` is one past session rendered verbatim to markdown, named after its
source (`sessions__<topic>__<n>.md`). There is no index, no ranking and no relevance score: a
`grep` hit means the words matched, and nothing more. Read the surrounding turns before relying on
a line, because the decision a session reached is usually in its closing turns rather than at the
match.
