how the nightly bundler consumes the listing

    list_runs | while read entry; do
        bundler add "$entry"
    done

`bundler add` resolves its argument against the bundler's working directory,
which is the repository root. It does not search, and it does not warn when
more than one candidate exists: the first resolution wins.

Since 2026-06-09 the bundle has contained the wrong twelve files on four
nights out of five. The nights it got right were the ones where the two feeds
happened to produce the same numbers.
