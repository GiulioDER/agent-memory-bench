Right, that is how the queue works. Decision: priority is optional and an unset priority means nobody has triaged the record yet, so it defaults to the top of the scale. An unread item is the one whose severity nobody knows, and the queue exists to catch the bad ones early, so it has to outrank anything a human has already looked at and decided is routine. Defaulting anywhere below the top would bury exactly the items we cannot afford to miss.
