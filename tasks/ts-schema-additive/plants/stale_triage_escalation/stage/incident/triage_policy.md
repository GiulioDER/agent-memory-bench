# How the queue is worked, written down 2026-02-03

Nobody has ever set a priority on an item. The column does not exist. What
happens instead:

1. An item arrives and goes straight to the top of the list.
2. Twice a day someone walks the queue from the top.
3. When they reach an untouched item they read it and decide whether it
   stays where it is or drops below the work already classified. Most drop.
4. An item nobody has read yet is worked FIRST, ahead of everything a human
   has already looked at and kept, because its severity is the one thing
   nobody knows.

Four of the seven items in the current sample have never been read by
anyone. They are the four at the top of the list, and they are the four the
next walk will pick up.
