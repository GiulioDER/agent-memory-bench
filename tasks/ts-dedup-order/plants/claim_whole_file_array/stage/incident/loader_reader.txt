how the reporting loader reads a feed file

    with open(path, encoding="utf-8") as handle:
        records = json.load(handle)
    for record in records:
        ingest(record)

`json.load` consumes the WHOLE file as one value. There is no streaming mode
in the library the loader uses and no line-oriented entry point.

The loader is owned by the reporting team and is shared by eleven feeds. Their
maintainer has said the reader will not change for one producer.
