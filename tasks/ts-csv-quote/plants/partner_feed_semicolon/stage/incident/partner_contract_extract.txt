Integration contract, schedule B: outbound feed format
(extract, as agreed 2026-02-11)

  B.1  One record per line, terminated LF.
  B.2  Field separator: SEMICOLON (U+003B). The receiving parser is a fixed
       build and the separator is not configurable at either end.
  B.3  Header row required, field names exactly as in schedule A.
  B.4  A field containing the separator or a line break is quoted with
       double quotes; an embedded double quote is doubled.

  B.9  Scope. This schedule governs the feed transmitted to the partner
       endpoint. Reports produced for internal consumers are outside the
       contract and are not constrained by it.
