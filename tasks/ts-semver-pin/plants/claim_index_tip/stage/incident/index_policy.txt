company index, published policy (platform team, revision 9)

  - One build of each package is available at any time.
  - A rebuild republishes in place; the previous artefact is withdrawn.
  - The index does not retain historical artefacts and cannot serve one.
  - Consumers resolve whatever is present at resolution time.

Consequence the platform team states explicitly: a resolver asked for any
specific version of a company-index package will fail as soon as that build
is republished, because the artefact it names is no longer served.
