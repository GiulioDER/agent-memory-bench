What makes a result official

or it does not appear here

A run appears on this page only when all of the following hold, in this order:

gaterequirement
1 · preregistered protocol committed under preregistration/ before the first session; the harness refuses to start while that directory is dirty
2 · announced the run is announced before it happens, not after it succeeds
3 · gated every scored cell passed the admission gate; discard counts published per arm
4 · published in full per-session logs, streams, admission verdicts and costs land in results/<run_id>/, wins and losses alike
Pilot and diagnostic runs

Smoke tests and pilots exist in the repository's results/ tree and are exactly what their names say: bring-up. They are not rankings, they are not previews of rankings, and they will not be quoted as either.

As of 2026-08-29 that tree holds four published pilot runs and one repair record, with one product arm and no third-party arm. Three ran on one cheap model; the fourth, on a stronger one, ran out of provider credit at 40 of its 72 cells and is incomplete rather than negative. Their per-session logs, admission verdicts and cost ledgers are published because the benchmark publishes what it runs, not because any of it is a result about the field.

It is also less than has been run. Abstention and diagnostic runs exist and are not committed, so nothing about them can be checked here and nothing from them is quoted. A benchmark whose rule is that results publish in full, win or lose, does not currently meet it, and the accounting of what is missing is in docs/STATUS.md.

One caveat that outlives them. Three graded-outcome defects were fixed on 2026-08-29: a grader that rejected correct solutions about 40% of the time, a checker crash that discarded a whole paired cell instead of failing one arm, and cost estimates that priced cache reads as fresh input. A run measured after those fixes is not protocol-identical to the pilots in this tree, so the two must not be differenced. The break is recorded, with the arithmetic, in the protocol change record, and where the benchmark stands overall is in docs/STATUS.md.
